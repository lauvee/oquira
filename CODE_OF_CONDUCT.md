# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in the Oquira community.

We welcome contributions regardless of age, background, experience, identity, nationality, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
- Provide and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect differing opinions and technical approaches.
- Help maintain a collaborative and inclusive environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate intimidation or threats.
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Oquira community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions.
- Issues and pull requests.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Public spaces when someone is representing Oquira.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through the project's designated communication channels or contact the maintainers privately.

Reports will be reviewed seriously and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, responses may include:

1. A warning.
2. Temporary restrictions from community participation.
3. Removal from project spaces.
4. Permanent exclusion from the community.

Maintainers will aim to act fairly and consistently.
