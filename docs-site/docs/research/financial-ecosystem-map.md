# Dominican Financial Ecosystem Map



**Version:** 0.1\

**Status:** Initial research map\

**Project:** Oquira\

**Geographic focus:** Dominican Republic\

**Related framework:** Dominican Open Finance Exploration Framework (DOFEF)



## 1. Purpose



This document establishes an initial map of the Dominican financial ecosystem.



Its purpose is to identify the institutions, participants, infrastructure, financial services and relationships that shape the country's financial environment.



The map provides a foundation for understanding where Open Banking, Open Finance, financial interoperability and emerging financial technologies could create new opportunities.



Oquira will use this research to design experiments involving traditional financial institutions, fintech companies, cooperatives, businesses, consumers and future financial participants.



This is a research document, not an official institutional classification or regulatory directory.



## 2. Research methodology



The ecosystem will be analyzed through six dimensions:



1\. Institutional landscape.

2\. Financial participants.

3\. Financial products and services.

4\. Financial infrastructure.

5\. Financial data and interoperability.

6\. Innovation opportunities.



### 2.1 Classification of findings



Every relevant finding should use one of the following classifications:



| Classification | Meaning |

|---|---|

| Fact | Supported by an identifiable source. |

| Announced | Publicly announced but not necessarily operational. |

| In development | An initiative or infrastructure being developed. |

| Hypothesis | An assumption requiring further investigation. |

| Proposal | A potential solution or model. |

| Simulation | A hypothetical model implemented inside Oquira. |



The distinction is essential to avoid presenting proposed or experimental capabilities as existing financial infrastructure.

### Experimental opportunity



**Connected Cooperative**



Oquira could simulate multiple independent cooperatives that expose standardized financial information through APIs.



An authorized application could demonstrate how a member might visualize accounts and loans across participating cooperatives.



This would be a simulation, not a representation of an existing nationwide cooperative interoperability network.

## 17. Guiding principle



Oquira must understand the Dominican financial ecosystem as it exists today while maintaining the freedom to explore how it could evolve.



The purpose of this map is not to reproduce the existing financial system.



It is to understand its participants, relationships, infrastructure and limitations so that new possibilities can be explored responsibly.



**Understand the ecosystem. Connect its participants. Experiment with its possibilities.**
