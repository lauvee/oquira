# Dominican Republic Landscape

## Research objective

Understand the financial, regulatory, and technological context for Open Banking and Open Finance in the Dominican Republic.

## Research areas

1. Regulatory initiatives and public statements.
2. Role of the Superintendencia de Bancos.
3. Data protection and financial confidentiality.
4. Banking ecosystem and digital services.
5. Existing interoperability initiatives.
6. Potential participants and use cases.
7. Opportunities and implementation challenges.

## Preliminary findings

The following findings are based on publicly available
institutional sources and should be reviewed as the
regulatory and technical landscape evolves.

- The Superintendencia de Bancos has publicly documented
  initiatives to explore Open Banking in the Dominican Republic.
- Public discussions involving financial regulators,
  fintech organizations, and innovation stakeholders
  have addressed Open Finance opportunities.
- The Superintendencia de Bancos provides public APIs
  for financial system statistics. These should not be
  confused with customer-permissioned Open Banking APIs.
- Dominican legislation includes provisions concerning
  personal data protection and financial confidentiality.
- The regulatory and technical requirements for a
  production Open Finance ecosystem require further research.

These findings do not establish that a complete Open
Finance regulatory framework or production API standard
is currently in force.

## Initial questions

- What initiatives related to Open Banking have been publicly discussed?
- Which institutions and stakeholders are involved?
- What rules govern financial data confidentiality?
- What requirements apply to personal data processing?
- Are there published API or interoperability standards?
- What would be required for a regulated implementation?
