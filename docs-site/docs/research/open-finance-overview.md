# Open Finance Overview

## What is Open Finance?

Open Finance refers to frameworks and practices that enable consumers and businesses to share financial information with authorized third parties through secure and controlled mechanisms.

The objective is to support innovation, competition, interoperability, and improved financial experiences.

## Open Banking and Open Finance

Open Banking generally focuses on payment accounts and banking information.

Open Finance can extend data-sharing concepts to a broader range of financial products and services.

## Main actors

- **ASPSP:** Account Servicing Payment Service Provider.
- **TPP:** Third Party Provider.
- **AISP:** Account Information Service Provider.
- **PISP:** Payment Initiation Service Provider.
- **Customer:** The individual or business authorizing access to financial data.

These roles may have different legal meanings depending on the applicable framework.

## Core concepts

- Consent and authorization.
- Authentication.
- Scoped access.
- API interoperability.
- Secure data transmission.
- Revocation and expiration.
- Auditability.

## Standards and frameworks to investigate

### Authorization and identity protocols

- OAuth 2.0.
- OpenID Connect.

### Financial API security profiles

- Financial-grade API (FAPI).

### Open Banking API specifications

- UK Open Banking Standards.
- Berlin Group NextGenPSD2.

These technologies and specifications serve different purposes.
Their applicability depends on the intended use case and
the regulatory framework.
