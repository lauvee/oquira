# Oquira Product Vision

## Vision

Oquira is an open-source experimental platform for exploring Open Finance, financial data sharing, and consent management, initially focused on the Dominican Republic.

Its purpose is to provide a practical environment to understand, experiment with, and demonstrate the technical and product concepts behind Open Finance.

## Problem

Financial information is often distributed across multiple institutions, making it difficult for individuals to obtain a unified view of their financial position.

Open Finance introduces mechanisms that can enable secure, consent-driven sharing of financial information between institutions and authorized third parties.

The initial problem statement is a research hypothesis.
The project will investigate relevant user needs, existing
financial infrastructure, and potential opportunities before
committing to specific product solutions.

## Objectives

- Explore Open Finance concepts and architecture.
- Simulate financial institutions and their APIs.
- Demonstrate consent and authorization workflows.
- Aggregate synthetic financial accounts and transactions.
- Provide a developer-oriented API and documentation.
- Explore responsible applications of AI and LLMs.

## Initial scope

All consent and authorization workflows are simulated
for educational and technical demonstration purposes.
They do not represent legally valid banking consent or
authorization to access real financial accounts.

The first version will use synthetic data and simulated financial institutions.

No real bank integrations or real customer financial information will be used.

## Non-goals

- Operating as a bank or financial institution.
- Providing real financial services.
- Claiming regulatory certification or official institutional affiliation.
- Accessing real banking credentials or accounts.

## Disclaimer

Oquira is an independent experimental and educational project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any bank, financial institution, or regulator.
