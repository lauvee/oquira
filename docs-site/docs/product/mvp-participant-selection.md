# MVP Participant Selection

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the selection strategy for institutional participants represented in the initial Oquira MVP.

Oquira will maintain a broad research registry while implementing a smaller, representative sample of financial ecosystem participants.

The objective is sector coverage and meaningful interoperability experiments, not exhaustive institutional representation.

## 2. Selection principles

Participants will be evaluated according to:

- Relevance in the Dominican financial ecosystem.
- Sector representativeness.
- Diversity of business models.
- Potential Open Finance use cases.
- Availability of public institutional evidence.
- Value for interoperability experiments.
- Feasibility of building a representative simulation.

Selection does not imply partnership, endorsement, API access or integration with the real institution.

## 3. Participant representation models

Each participant must use one of these representation models:

| Model | Description |
|---|---|
| Real verified | Real institution with documented identity and institutional status |
| Real experimental profile | Real institution whose capabilities or integrations are simulated |
| Synthetic archetype | Fictional participant representing a business model or emerging financial service |

A real institution must never be presented as operating an API or capability that has not been independently verified.

## 6. Proposed MVP capabilities

The initial portfolio should support experiments involving:

1. Financial account aggregation.
2. Balance and transaction retrieval.
3. Consent creation, consultation and revocation.
4. Payment initiation simulation.
5. Digital wallet and payment-provider interoperability.
6. Cooperative financial data access.
7. Alternative credit assessment.
8. Insurance policy information exchange.
9. Investment and securities portfolio aggregation.
10. Pension information aggregation.
11. Remittance tracking and financial planning.
12. Business financial data exchange.

Not every participant needs to implement every capability.

## 7. Selection and verification workflow

A candidate moves through these stages:

1. Identified: participant or institutional archetype has been identified.
2. Evidence collected: public source references have been recorded.
3. Identity verified: legal or institutional identity has been checked.
4. Status verified: relevant authorization or registration status has been checked.
5. MVP selected: participant has been approved for the initial scope.
6. Profile modeled: products, capabilities and data structures are defined.
7. Simulator implemented: synthetic behavior is available for experimentation.

A candidate may remain pending without blocking the MVP.

## 8. Exclusions and boundaries

The MVP will not:

- Attempt to reproduce every institution in the Dominican Republic.
- Claim real integrations where none exist.
- Treat directory presence as proof of operational status.
- Treat a payment registration as universal authorization for financial services.
- Present synthetic participants as real institutions.
- Infer partnerships or institutional endorsements.

## 9. Future expansion

The candidate portfolio can expand as Oquira develops.

Future research may include:

- Additional banks and financial intermediaries.
- More cooperatives and community finance organizations.
- Remittance operators and exchange agents.
- Insurance and pension providers.
- Investment funds and securities infrastructure.
- Fintech companies and payment institutions.
- Embedded finance and Banking-as-a-Service models.
- International participants and experimental archetypes.

The historical registry remains independent of the MVP participant selection.

## 10. Current decision

Oquira adopts the principle:

**Broad ecosystem coverage, selective institutional depth.**

The initial portfolio is a research shortlist, not a declaration of verified integrations or institutional relationships.

Final selection requires evidence review and explicit MVP scope approval.
