# Building the foundations of Open Finance.

**Exploring financial infrastructure. Advancing open technologies. Building in public.**

Oquira is an independent, open-source initiative exploring the technologies, architectures, and principles behind Open Finance.

Our goal is to investigate how financial data sharing, consent management, interoperable systems, and modern financial technologies can contribute to a more accessible, transparent, and connected financial ecosystem.

Initially focused on the Dominican Republic, Oquira combines technical research, architectural experimentation, and open-source development to explore practical approaches to financial interoperability.

---

## What is Oquira?

Oquira is a research and development initiative designed to explore the foundations of modern financial infrastructure.

The project investigates:

- **Open Finance:** Financial data sharing, interoperability, and emerging financial ecosystems.
- **Consent Management:** Secure, transparent, and user-centered authorization of financial data access.
- **Financial Infrastructure:** Modular architectures, APIs, and integration patterns.
- **Digital Innovation:** The responsible exploration of tokenization, artificial intelligence, and emerging financial technologies.

## Guiding principles

Oquira is built around a set of principles that guide its research and development:

- **Open by design:** Transparent development and open-source collaboration.
- **Privacy first:** Security and privacy considered from the beginning.
- **Interoperability:** Exploring standards-based approaches to financial connectivity.
- **Responsible experimentation:** Using synthetic data and simulated institutions.
- **Evidence-driven engineering:** Making incremental decisions supported by research and technical evaluation.
- **Technological responsibility:** Evaluating emerging technologies, including AI and tokenization, only when they provide a justified benefit and can be explored responsibly.

## Project status

**Current stage: Discovery and architecture definition.**

Oquira is currently focused on research, requirements analysis, architectural design, and the definition of its initial technical foundations.

The initial implementation will use simulated financial institutions, synthetic financial data, and controlled experimental environments.

No real banking credentials or production financial accounts are required or used in this stage.

## Disclaimer

Oquira is an independent experimental, educational, and research initiative.

It is not a bank, financial institution, regulated financial service, payment provider, or official Open Finance infrastructure. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any bank, financial institution, government authority, or financial regulator.

All initial demonstrations and experiments are conducted using simulated institutions and synthetic data.
